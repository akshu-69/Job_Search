# Job discovery criteria

US software IC roles: Java/Spring Boot, Java/React full stack, backend/API/microservices, some applied-AI integration. Massachusetts preferred but nationwide including relocation acceptable. Full time >=$85k annual salary max or >=$41/hr max when disclosed; no salary disclosure is fine. Original employer/agency posting must confirm active, full time, location, role, qualifications and posting age <=14 days; unknown dates go in date_unknown. Review ambiguous citizenship/clearance/export control/remote geographic scope/contract/non-IC leadership and substantial gaps. Exclude internships/new grad, Staff+, managers, unrelated core tech, explicit US citizen only, Crusoe, Fidelity/FMR and expired roles.

Do not guess any job URL, posting date, requisition ID or salary. Prioritize unique req IDs and avoid repeats across batches/days. Do not use crawl date as posting date. Store employer links, preserve board lead when possible. User personally applies.
